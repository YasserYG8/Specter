# strace source overhead, WSL2, 2026-11-09

Made with `scripts/bench-overhead.sh` (5 runs of 10 s after 2 s of warm-up, for each load, with and without Specter attached). The server is `test/e2e/testserver`, whose `/json` endpoint writes one log line to a pipe per request.
